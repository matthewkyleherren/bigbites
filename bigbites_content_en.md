## BigBites Platform
**Your restaurant. Your rules.**

Most platforms take a bite out of your profit. We’d rather help you grow it.
BigBites gives you full control of your online business — from at-table mobile orders to delivery integration — without unfair cuts or hidden strings.

- **Built for all service types.** Handle takeaway, delivery, and in-restaurant orders (via kiosks or your guests’ phones) in one place.
- **Connect anywhere.** Use your own drivers, or link into services like Uber Eats or eats.ch when it suits you.
- **Integrate easily.** Add online ordering to your existing website via our web components — no redesign needed.
- **Or go custom.** We build unique, responsive websites tailored to your brand, design, and audience.

BigBites is the digital backbone for restaurants that want independence, not interference.
